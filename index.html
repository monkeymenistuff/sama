Simple, research-backed techniques to help you focus, remember more, and feel confident before your next exam.

Better habits, better results.

Small changes to your study routine can make a big difference.

⏱️

The Pomodoro Method

Study for 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break. After four sessions, take a longer break to recharge.

🧠

Active Recall

Close your notes and test yourself on what you remember. Retrieving information strengthens your memory.

📅

Spaced Repetition

Review material over several days instead of cramming everything into one long session.

📝

Practice Questions

Use past papers and practice problems to find knowledge gaps and get comfortable with exam formats.

📵

Reduce Distractions

Silence notifications, keep your desk tidy, and choose one clear goal before each study session.

💤

Prioritise Sleep

Getting enough sleep supports concentration, learning, and memory consolidation.

✨ A simple daily study routine

Start with a 5-minute plan. Work on your hardest subject while you're fresh. Take regular breaks, quiz yourself on what you've learned, and finish by writing down your next steps for tomorrow.
